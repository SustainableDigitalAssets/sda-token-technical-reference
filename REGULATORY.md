# Regulatory identity

SDA is supervised under MiCA. The competent authority is the Finnish Financial
Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA). SDA is neither an asset-referenced token (ART) nor an
e-money token (EMT). Sustainable Digital Assets Inc. holds no crypto-asset service
provider (CASP) authorisation.

- Issuer: Sustainable Digital Assets Inc.
- LEI: `89450058XEES8WCSCQ03`
- Classification: MiCA Title II crypto-asset other than an ART or EMT (utility token)
- Competent authority: FIN-FSA
- White paper: version 1.3, notified under MiCA Article 8 and published on 2026-07-16 in inline XBRL
- Register identity: DTI `ZHSMZL7F9` (ISO 24165)
- Scope: 29 EEA states

Notification of a crypto-asset white paper under MiCA Article 8 is not an approval of the crypto-asset or of the issuer by FIN-FSA or any other competent authority.
